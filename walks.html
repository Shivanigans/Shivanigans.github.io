Two walks, drawn as they were recorded

Every plate shares one scale, so a bigger plate means a longer walk. Circles are pauses, sized by how long the standing still lasted, on the same scale across every plate. The profile beneath each plate is the climb.

Where the line runs straight, Strava had stopped recording but the walking carried on. The long dash across naddi is a cab — part of the journey, but not part of the distance walked.

Orange shapes are real buildings and roads from OpenStreetMap, and the small firs mark wooded ground. Both walks climb out of forest into a town, and you can see where one gives way to the other.
