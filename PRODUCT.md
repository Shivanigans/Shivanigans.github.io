## Platform

## Users

Three confirmed audiences, in no ranked order:

- **Policy and think-tank hirers** assessing a candidate for design work on research output.
- **Freelance clients** deciding whether to commission work.
- **Tech and product employers** hiring a product designer.

Their job on the site is to judge credibility and fit quickly, then verify credentials.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Shivani, a product designer. It exists to show real work and route a qualified visitor to her credentials.

Success means a visitor moves on to her LinkedIn profile or CV. Email remains available as a secondary route.

2. Speak as a product designer, not a developer.

5. Keep the site static, light and fully public-safe.
