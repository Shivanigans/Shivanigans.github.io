About

Hi, I'm Shivani. I draw and make things, and when I do, I like them to be thoughtful and human.

My practice sits at the intersection of visual storytelling and UX information design. I make sense of the world through comics, data-viz and sketchnoting.

I've worked as a data communications person for a think tank in the climate space, and before that as a designer at Revisual Labs and in user research.

I volunteer with VizChitra, India's first data visualisation conference and community. That one is purely for the fun of it.

Recently I've been venturing into building my own projects. On the side, I'm focused on my zine collection, map collection and rock collection.
